# Splitwise product audit

Checked 2026-09-14 to 2026-09-16 against splitwise.com, the feedback board, and press.

## What Splitwise has

| Area | Feature | Market |
|---|---|---|
| Core | Groups (Trip, Home, Couple, Other), expenses, simplify debts, comments, recurring expenses, search (Pro), default splits (Pro), charts (Pro), currency conversion (Pro), receipt scanning with itemization (Pro) | Global |
| Pro | Individual $59.99/yr, Duo $89.99/yr, 7-day trial. Free tier capped at about 3 expenses per day with a wait between adds | Global |
| Pro | Transaction import: connect a credit or debit card, see recent purchases, split with a tap | US only |
| Pay | Splitwise Pay: link a US checking account, send payments, wallet, withdraw. Bank via Coastal Community Bank | US only |
| Card | Splitwise Card: Mastercard debit on the Pay wallet, auto-adds purchases, optional auto-split, no Pro needed | US only |
| Settle | Venmo and PayPal deep links | US |
| Settle | Pay by Bank via Tink: UK (April 2024), then France, Germany, Austria (October 2025) | UK, EU |
| India | Paytm integration (2017, Android only). No UPI settle-up. "Record a payment" only | India |

## What it does not have

- Any settlement path in India. The UPI request has 458 votes and has been "under review" since 2017.
- Pooling money before a trip. The request has 59 votes since 2014. The official answer is "add a fake member called Pool."
- Applying a payment to a specific expense. 985 votes, the top request on the board.
- Adding friends without an email or phone. 625 votes.
- Scheduled or automatic settlement.
- An official MCP or AI connector.

## Business context

- Last raise: $20M Series A, April 28, 2021, led by Insight Partners. About $9M to $10M before that across seed rounds from 2012 to 2018. About $30.5M total. No round since.
- About 50 people. Providence, RI, plus US remote.
- The 2023 free-tier cap, ads, and the 2026 Pro price increase read as a push to self-fund.
- 2021 ambition: "the global platform for interpersonal finance."
- JD language (2026): "evaporate awkward conversations about money," "novel multi-player financial experiences," success is when a customer says Splitwise saved a relationship.

## Product Manager role, from the JD

- Generalist PM, reports to the CEO. Owns ideas end to end. Works with design, data, engineering, compliance.
- Mandate: "brand new initiatives, growth features, and fintech projects in 2026 and beyond."
- Wants: 3+ years, empathy, scrappy and strategic, fact-based, strong writing, intellectual honesty.
- Valued: consumer, fintech, global experience; deep knowledge of the Splitwise product; SQL; experimentation; "tasteful appreciation for AI-enabled workflows where benefits exceed costs/risks."
- Apply by email to jobs@splitwise.com with a one-page PDF resume and answers to four questions. Do not use AI to write the email.
- Posting is on splitwise.com/jobs and syndicated to the Insight Partners and Inspired Capital portfolio boards. Not on LinkedIn.
