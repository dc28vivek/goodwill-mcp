# Interview guide

Goal: five to eight conversations with people who organize Splitwise groups. Twenty minutes each. Record with permission. Write notes the same day.

## Who to talk to

- People who created a trip group in the last six months.
- People who run a roommate group.
- One couple who use it daily.
- Prefer people who are the "organizer" in their group. Ask: "Who adds most of the expenses in your group?" If they say "me," they are the person.

## Questions

Ask them in order. Follow the energy. Do not pitch.

1. Tell me about the last group you set up. What happened from the first expense to the last payment?
2. Who adds the expenses? How do you remember what to add?
3. When was the last time someone asked you why they owed what they owed? What did you do?
4. Tell me about the last time someone was late paying you back. What did you do? How did it feel?
5. Have you ever hit the daily limit? What did you do next?
6. Have you ever posted the same expense twice? How did you find out?
7. If you could say one sentence to Splitwise and it would do the rest, what would the sentence be?
8. What would make you nervous about an AI adding expenses to your group?

## What to listen for

- The exact words they use for entry, explaining, and chasing. Those words become tool descriptions.
- The moment they say "awkward." Note what caused it.
- Anything they do outside the app: spreadsheets, WhatsApp messages, screenshots.
